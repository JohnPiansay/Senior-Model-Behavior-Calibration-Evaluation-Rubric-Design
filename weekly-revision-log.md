# Weekly Rubric Revision Log

Purpose: track one evidence-backed rubric dimension review per week. A proposed change is not an approved revision. Do not backfill work that did not occur.

## Weekly operating cycle

1. Collect anonymized disagreements, evaluator feedback, benchmark errors, and policy clarifications from the prior week.
2. Identify one dimension to review; state the observed ambiguity or failure pattern and link permitted evidence.
3. Draft a wording/anchor change and contrasting good, borderline, and bad examples.
4. Compare old and proposed criteria on a representative set; note score changes, regressions, and unresolved policy questions.
5. Obtain evaluation-lead/policy-owner approval. Record version, approver, effective date, affected cases, and communication plan.
6. Re-score impacted work if the approved change materially changes a label; retain old and new scores.

## Change record template

| Week/date | Dimension | Evidence or dispute IDs | Problem statement | Proposed text change | Exemplars updated | Impact / re-score | Decision and dissent | Approver / effective version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not completed | Not assigned | No team cases supplied | Initial draft only; requires team calibration before production use | None approved | Synthetic examples only | Not assessed | Pending team review | Proposed v1.0; no approver/date supplied |

## Current status

No weekly revision cycle has been performed or represented as complete in this starter package. Populate a dated row only after the review, evidence assessment, and approval have actually occurred.
